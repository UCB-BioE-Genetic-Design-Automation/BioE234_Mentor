## Getting the right abstraction level

When working with an LLM to write code, the first thing you must understand is that it follows what you say exactly, but it will fill in the blanks of what you leave out. It doesn't have "common sense" like a person, nor agency, nor memory of your previous chats. Thus, you need to be careful in saying exactly what you want and avoiding saying what you don't want.

Open up a new chat (not just a new message in this chat, but a new one. For this, you can use any AI chatbot like Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude). In the new chat, copy and paste this and submit it:

'''
Write code that chooses an RBS for a gene
'''

Copy whatever it spits out back to me.
