## Examine the Signature

OK, we got some code, let's examine what we have.

The signature of a function is its name, the inputs it expects, and what it returns. It also describes the shape of those inputs, meaning what kind of data each one is (like text, a number, or an object) and how many inputs there are. It also includes the names the function will use for those inputs inside the function.

So, what is the signature of this function? What is its name, what are the inputs and their type, and what is the type of the output?
